The files in this folder are the woff2 subsets Google Fonts serves for the two families (latin for Outfit
and Heebo, hebrew for Heebo), copied here so tools/brand/render-kit.js renders the kit with the real faces
on a machine without internet. They are used only to render the marketing kit; the app itself loads the
fonts from Google Fonts (index.html).
