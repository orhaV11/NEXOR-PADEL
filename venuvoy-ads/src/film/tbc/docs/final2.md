## The story of these five films

Vic asked his daughter to marry in the morning and she asked what time kick-off was, which nobody can tell her; the six tickets cannot leave his phone and he cannot leave the wedding, so the phone has to travel without him, and after seventy-five days of typing Danny sends four words: "Leave it with me." (Episode 5). The airline then needs a surname for every seat and, after eleven years, nobody in the chat has Danny's: he is "Danny Football" in every phone, and the one person who knows it is not in the chat (Short A). Maya booked seven seats and four people fly, in T-shirts that are right for one day: Vic is at a wedding, Lee found it cheaper (three flights and a bus, arriving "before kick-off, depending on kick-off"), Danny has no surname, and the phone with the six tickets is in a floral armchair, because Danny left it with his grandmother, who does not answer a phone that is not hers (Episode 6). The day before, the kick-off time is confirmed at last, in green, and moved within three seconds for television, to a time that suits only Sydney, who is not going, and that turns Lee's route into an arrival at half-time (Short B). On match day the four who did everything properly are at the gate and learn where their tickets are from Sydney, who has seen Danny's grandmother on television: she is in her seat, early, with Vic's phone ringing in her hand ("That'll be for Vic."), Danny is behind her, and Vic is recorded as attending his twelfth match from his daughter's wedding (Episode 7). The season ends where the last one did, at greater expense and with everyone content: two got in, one is not in the chat and the other is typing, and the study stamps it CONFIRMED on the same kind of frozen frame that was stamped TO BE CONFIRMED in Episode 1; Lee arrives at half-time having missed nothing, on Friday Vic pays and OWE MAYA moves for the first time, to 5, Danny at last answers the question the chat is named after ("I'm in.") and stops typing, Jo's verdict is "It was fine.", the grandmother's status is WENT., and the last line is "Next season: to be confirmed."
