# The rig: what Luna and Timo can do today, and what a new thing costs

Read this before writing or building a sketch. The first sketch made this way is "The Switch"
(`ads/20-sketches/venuvoy-sketch-01-the-switch-en_9x16_1080x1920.mp4`; its script is in `src/film/reset/RESET.md`,
its picture in `src/film/toon/switch_film.py`). The founder saw it and asked for many more ("run episodes, lots, so
there is content to upload").

## The format: Luna's trip vlog

Luna films herself with her phone held at arm's length (a selfie vlog, 9:16) on a sports trip. Timo is with her,
standing behind her on our right. We never leave her phone: every shot is that selfie view, in a new place, wider or
closer. Cuts are vlog cuts (a hard jump cut, or a quick whip when time has passed). Under the first shots plays "the
music she put on her vlog"; it can stop dead on a look.

- A **sketch** is one situation and one joke: 15 to 22 seconds, at most 11 short spoken lines, then the brand plate
  (VENUVOY, "one sentence. your sports trip, planned.") for 2.4 s with the sung name.
- A **short** is one beat: 6 to 10 seconds, 2 to 4 lines, one shot (two at most), a 1.3 s plate.

Each line is also a caption (hers white, his teal), one caption on screen at a time. There is no other text: no
labels, no typed sentence, no product card.

## What Luna can do (drawn and working)

Seen from her phone: head and shoulders, left of centre; yellow raincoat, dark-green and cream scarf, orange bob.
One arm holds the phone we look through; the other is free.

- Faces: **sunny** (open smile, eyes on us; her resting face), **guilty** (worried brows, a sideways look toward Timo,
  a crooked closed smile), **caught** (big worried eyes on us, a small pout), **reading** (eyes down at her phone,
  pleased; her eyes move along the line), **looking down** (eyes down beside her, mouth shut), **oh** (eyes down, a
  small round mouth of surprise), **turned to him** (her whole head turns to Timo and looks up at him). She blinks on
  every one of them.
- She talks on all of them (four mouth shapes; three on the turned head).
- Her head tilts into her lines, dips on stressed words, ducks when caught, bows to her phone.
- Her free hand can come up from below with: **her phone** (she reads something off it) or **an open hand**
  ("what do you want from me"). A new thing in that hand is one new drawing (a ticket, a cup, a foam finger, a map,
  a sandwich, a medal, a small flag with no emblem).
- Outdoors her hair and scarf blow in the wind. Indoors needs one new drawing of her with calm hair (then all her
  faces work on it).

## What Timo can do (drawn and working)

Standing behind her on our right, head and chest, hands in his pockets, teal track jacket, the same scarf, flat-top
hair. Dry, proud, clever. He never mugs.

- Eyes: **on her** (heavy lids, looking down at her; his resting look), **on us** (a flat look into the lens: his
  signature, best after a line of hers, with the music cut), **narrowed** (one brow down: he is working something
  out), **shut** (he has given up). He blinks.
- He talks (three mouth shapes), leans a little toward her while he speaks, pulls his head back when talked over.
- He can **sink** straight down out of the frame (slowly, eyes shut), and **come up** into it the same way. He can be
  absent from a shot and speak from outside it.
- From below the frame his **raised finger** can come up (an objection), wag on his words, and tip over and sink.
- On grass he can **lie face down** far behind her and lift his head a little to speak (one drawing exists, outdoors
  on a slope only).
- Something he **wears or carries** is one new drawing of the same pose with one change (a silly hat, sunglasses, a
  rain poncho, ear defenders, a medal, a giant foam hand on his head). Deadpan Timo in a ridiculous thing is cheap
  and strong. His face must stay free (eyes and mouth are separate drawings).

## What a place costs

One painted background per place (a stadium concourse, a grandstand at a race track, a tennis court's back row, a
golf fairway, a street on match day, a station platform). No readable signs, no real club, league, sponsor or city.
People in the distance are fine as soft shapes; nobody else is drawn close and nobody else speaks (a crowd, an
announcer or a passing engine can be HEARD).

## What cannot be done (do not write it)

Walking or running in view, the two of them touching or handing things over, a third character with a face or a
line, any view that is not her selfie view, a close-up of an object with small writing, a readable screen, real
names of clubs, leagues, players, stadiums or sponsors, anything typed on screen.

## Budget of a sketch

Voices about 15 cents, a background 2 cents, each other new drawing 2 cents, one bought ambience 1.5 cents, checks
about 15 cents: about 60 cents a sketch, 15 cents a short. The cap is 5 dollars a film. Plan for at most 2
backgrounds and 4 other new drawings in a sketch; 1 background and 1 new drawing in a short.

## What the first sketch taught (two rounds of independent checking)

- The first second must stop a thumb WITHOUT sound: her face close, something odd behind her.
- With the sound off it must always be clear who speaks: when Timo is out of the frame, something of his (his
  finger) must be on screen with his caption, and Luna's mouth must be shut.
- A beat needs air: 0.3 to 0.45 s before a turn, a stare of 0.75 s, a retort after 0.05 s, an interruption that runs
  0.2 s under the other voice. Do not cut the pause inside a punchline.
- Anything that leaves the picture must be seen leaving (0.7 s, at an even speed), with half a second of stillness
  first.
- Every cut must change the picture clearly: wider or closer by at least 15 per cent, or a whip.
- A stretch of more than about 3 seconds on one face with nothing new is where a viewer leaves.
- Timo is the only Black character: he is the clever one, his objections are dignified, and he must not always end
  on the ground. Let him win, and let him be on his feet at the end, in most sketches.
- Luna must read as a grown woman: adult lines, adult concerns; nothing nursery.
- Say "sports trip"; a sketch about one sport names that sport once. The film that exists is about football: the
  next ones are other sports.
- Product truth is in `src/film/s2/docs/venuvoy_facts.md`. Venuvoy PLANS (plan, build, find, compare, check, track).
  Never book, buy, sell, reserve, flight, hotel, price, "AI". In "The Switch" the true fact was that the planner has
  switches for hikes and runs. A sketch does not need a product fact at all: the plate at the end is enough.
